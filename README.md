# 🎮 G-GAMES

**G-GAMES** is a free, browser-based mini game hub where you can play many quick games and challenge an AI opponent, with nothing to download or install.

🔗 **Play now:** https://bryangablao.github.io/G-GAMES

---

## 📖 Overview

G-GAMES brings a variety of mini games together on one website. Instead of searching for a different site for every game, you open G-GAMES, pick a game, and start playing right away. Many of the games let you play against an AI, so you can enjoy them even when no one else is around.

The site is built to be fast, simple, and easy to use on both desktop and mobile.

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal of G-GAMES is to give players a quick, free, and fun way to pass the time:

- **Instant fun** – open the link and play within seconds
- **Play anytime, solo** – an AI opponent means you never need a second player
- **All in one place** – many mini games under a single website
- **Accessible for everyone** – runs in any modern browser, on any device, with no account or install needed
- **Sharpen your skills** – quick rounds help improve reaction time, strategy, and decision making

## 👥 Who Is It For?

- Players who want a quick game during a short break
- Friends and family looking for something simple to play together
- Anyone who enjoys beating a computer opponent
- Casual gamers who don't want to download or sign up for anything

## ✨ Features

- 🕹️ **Numerous mini games** – a growing collection of simple, easy-to-learn games
- 🤖 **Play against AI** – challenge a computer opponent in supported games
- ⚡ **No installation** – everything runs directly in your web browser
- 🆓 **Free to play** – no sign-up, no payment
- 📱 **Responsive design** – works on phones, tablets, and computers
- 🔄 **Replay anytime** – restart a game instantly and try to beat your last result

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Open the website: https://bryangablao.github.io/G-GAMES
2. Choose a game from the game list
3. Read the quick instructions for that game
4. Play against the AI (or on your own, depending on the game)
5. Restart and try again to improve your score or beat the AI

## 💡 Tips

- Take a moment to read each game's instructions before you start
- Start with easier games to learn the controls, then try tougher ones
- Replay the same game to learn how the AI plays and find its weaknesses
- On mobile, hold your phone in the orientation that fits the game best

## ❓ FAQ

**Is G-GAMES free?**
Yes. All games are free to play.

**Do I need to create an account?**
No. Just open the website and play.

**Can I play on my phone?**
Yes. The website is designed to work on mobile devices as well as computers.

**Do I need to download anything?**
No. Everything runs in your browser.

**Can I play with another person?**
Many games are designed for playing against the AI. Check each game's instructions for the available modes.
